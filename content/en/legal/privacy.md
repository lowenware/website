## What data we collect

When you submit the project inquiry form, we collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your message

We also require you to confirm that you have read this privacy statement before
submitting.

## How we use your data

We use inquiry submissions to:

- Respond to your message
- Evaluate whether we can help with your project
- Follow up on your inquiry by email

We do not use your data for marketing without your consent.

## Where your data is processed

Inquiry form submissions are sent to our API at my.lowenware.com and processed
by Löwenware s.r.o. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## How long we keep your data

We retain inquiry messages for as long as needed to handle your request and
maintain a record of our business correspondence. If you want your data
deleted, contact us at
[inbox@lowenware.com](mailto:inbox@lowenware.com).

## Your rights

access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict
certain processing. Contact
[inbox@lowenware.com](mailto:inbox@lowenware.com) to exercise these rights.

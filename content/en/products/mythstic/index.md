Mythstic is Löwenware's first game project. It is currently in the early
concept-writing stage — we are defining the world, mechanics, and narrative
before moving into production.

## Built on Dotrix

Mythstic will be powered by Dotrix, our in-house Rust 3D engine. Building our
own engine lets us make architectural decisions tailored to the game's needs
rather than working around a third-party framework.

## Status

This is not a commercial product yet. There is no release date, no preorder,
and no public demo. We share it here as part of our direction toward game
development — funded by the client engineering work we do today.

If you are interested in following our progress, watch this space or follow us
on [GitHub](https://github.com/dotrix-rs).

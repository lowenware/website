Löwenbooks is a digital publishing marketplace connecting independent authors
with readers searching for new voices. We built it to make publishing more
accessible and sustainable through fair royalties, transparent book purchases,
and thoughtful discovery shaped by its community.

For readers, it offers a calm way to discover and purchase digital books
without subscription pools or overwhelming marketplace noise. Each purchase
directly supports the author behind the story.

For writers, Löwenbooks provides an approachable path to digital publishing,
real reader feedback, and a meaningful share of every sale. The platform is
still growing, so early authors and readers can help shape what comes next.

Our goal is to grow a friendly, welcoming community where writers and readers
can connect, exchange thoughtful feedback, and help great stories find their
audience. Whether someone is publishing their first book or searching for a
new favourite author, we want them to feel at home.

Visit [lowenbooks.cz](https://lowenbooks.cz) to explore the marketplace.

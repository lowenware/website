Dotrix is Löwenware's in-house 3D engine, written in Rust. The name derives
from _dot_ and _matrix_ — keystones of rendering. We built it to power our own
game projects and open-sourced it because the solutions we develop can be
useful to others.

## Why we built it

Commercial game engines are powerful but come with licensing constraints and
abstraction layers that do not always fit our needs. Dotrix gives us direct
control over the rendering pipeline, ECS architecture, and platform support.

## For developers

Dotrix is modular and designed for Rust developers who want a modern 3D engine
without leaving the Rust ecosystem. Visit [dotrix.rs](https://dotrix.rs/) for
documentation, examples, and the source repository.

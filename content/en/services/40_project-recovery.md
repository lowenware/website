## What we do

We take over projects with missed deadlines, unstable codebases, or teams that
have lost momentum. We assess the situation openly — including AI-assisted
codebase analysis as part of our audit workflow — propose a recovery plan, and
start delivering in the first sprint.

## Typical engagements

- Codebase audits and technical due diligence
- Stabilization of failing or delayed deliveries
- Firmware and embedded system migrations
- Architecture modernization and technical debt reduction

## Our approach

The more we know about your situation, the better we can help. We do not
overpromise — we define a realistic path, prove value quickly, and let you
decide how to continue based on results.

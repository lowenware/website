## What we do

We build and maintain software where applications meet hardware: mobile device
managers, cloud backends for connected products, protocol converters, and
embedded web interfaces. AI-assisted prototyping helps us validate ideas faster
before committing to production implementations.

## Typical engagements

- Mobile apps for device configuration and monitoring
- Bluetooth Low Energy stacks and device communication
- Cloud-to-device messaging and fleet management
- Embedded web UIs for hardware products

## Relevant experience

We have supported IoT manufacturers, 3D printer ecosystems, and industrial
monitoring platforms — always at the intersection of firmware, protocols, and
user-facing software.

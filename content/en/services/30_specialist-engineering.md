## What we do

When standard web development is not enough, we bring deep systems expertise.
Our team maintains [Dotrix](/en/dotrix), an open-source Rust 3D engine, and applies that
knowledge to client projects with demanding performance requirements. AI assists
with profiling and analysis; the engineering decisions stay human-led.

## Typical engagements

- Rust application and systems development
- Graphics pipelines and rendering optimization
- Performance profiling and architecture review
- Porting or rewriting performance-critical components

## Open-source depth

[Dotrix](/en/dotrix) and our low-level systems work are public evidence of our engineering
standards. We build in the open when it serves the community and our clients.

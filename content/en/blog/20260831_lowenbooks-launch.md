We are excited to announce the official launch of
[Löwenbooks](https://lowenbooks.cz/en/), our new e-book marketplace where
authors can publish their work and readers can discover stories that matter.

This launch marks an important milestone for Löwenware. What began as an idea
has grown into a production platform with real book sales, payments, and
royalties for authors.

## Built for authors and readers

Löwenbooks gives independent authors the tools to publish and sell their books
while offering readers a simple way to find and enjoy new writing. The platform
includes book profiles with detailed metadata, support for illustrations and
translations, content warnings, and practical publishing guides.

We would like to thank everyone who tested the platform, shared feedback, and
helped us reach this point.

Visit [Löwenbooks](https://lowenbooks.cz/en/) to explore the available books or
publish your own. We look forward to seeing the stories that find their home
there.

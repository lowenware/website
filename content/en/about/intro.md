Löwenware is a senior software engineering studio based in Liberec, Czechia.
Since 2017 we have helped product teams deliver technically demanding software
across web platforms, mobile applications, connected devices, and embedded
systems.

We are a small team of senior developers and artists. Our skills span
frontends, backends, databases, networking, 3D visualizations, and firmware.

At the same time, we are covering a wide range of digital content creation, including photography, design, audio and video recording.

## Our goals

Client engineering remains our primary business — it funds everything we build.
Alongside that work, we are developing our own products in areas we are passionate about, like music,books, digital art and gamedevelopment.

## Engagement process

1. **Discovery** — We learn about your project, constraints, and goals.
2. **Team and scope** — We agree on team composition, engagement model, and
   rates.
3. **Integration** — We join your existing team or start delivery in agile,
   sprint-driven cycles.
4. **First sprint** — You evaluate our work at the end of the first sprint or
   month before committing further.

## Trial period

After the first sprint or month, you decide whether to continue, adjust the
engagement, or stop. Each sprint delivers working functionality, so you can
evaluate our skills and output with evidence.

After the first month, we ask for at least one month's notice to end an
engagement.

## Development in crisis

Sometimes we take over projects with missed deadlines or unstable codebases. We
have helped clients through these situations before. The more openly you share
your situation, the more effective our recovery plan will be.
